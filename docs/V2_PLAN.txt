ERIC DA RED - VERSION 2 IMPROVEMENT PLAN
=========================================

Backup of v1
- git tag  v1.0  and branch  backup/v1  (the exact live version)
- zip copy: ..\Eric_Da_Red_Backups\Eric_Da_Red_v1.0.zip
- v2 work happens on the  v2  branch. Nothing is pushed live until you say so.

What v1 is missing (found while reading the code)
- Battles have no music of their own: battle/boss slots are empty, so fights replay the map song.
- Every hit makes roughly the same sound. Nothing is panned or pitch-varied, and there's no room/reverb.
- No musical "transitions": entering a fight, winning, losing, chapter cards and rests are all a plain fade + one beep.
- Enemy turns are random surprises, so there's not much strategy beyond "attack".
- Blocking is pass/fail only. A great reaction isn't rewarded.
- The camera never moves except for shake. Killing blows don't land with any weight.


PART 1 - MUSIC (new js/music.js: an original adaptive soundtrack, synthesized live)
- Three new battle themes, all original, in a Nordic minor/dorian style (war drums, frame drum,
  drone like a tagelharpa, horn lead, choir pads):
    battle  - "Quartermaster's March" (140 bpm, driving)   all normal fights
    boss    - "Toll & Trouble"        (heavier, half-time) Cousin Sven
    kraken  - "The Backorder"         (dark choir, storm)  final boss
- ADAPTIVE LAYERS: at Madness >= 70 a rage layer (extra war drums, distorted bass) fades in.
  During RED FURY the whole mix goes full intensity. Low HP adds a heartbeat pulse.
- TRANSITION STINGERS (short musical pieces):
    battle start (war horn + drum hit), victory fanfare, defeat dirge, level-up flourish,
    chapter-card horn, rest/camp lyre, treasure jingle.
- Real song files still win: put an mp3 in config.js (battle/boss) and it's used instead.

PART 2 - COMBAT SOUND EFFECTS
- A new sound bus with stereo panning: sounds come from where they happen on screen.
- Light reverb so hits sit in a space instead of beeping in a void.
- Random pitch/level variation on every hit so repeated chops never sound copy-pasted.
- New per-material impacts: wood crack + splinter, paper rip, rope snap, flesh thud,
  tentacle squelch, metal clang for blocks, ringing PARRY, crit "boom-ting", heavy body fall,
  and a separate death sound for each enemy type.

PART 3 - GAMEPLAY
- ENEMY INTENTS: each enemy shows what it will do next turn (attack + rough damage,
  madness attack, or heal) above its health bar. Brace / Block / kill order become real choices.
- PERFECT PARRY: block in the first instant of the "!" window and you take 0 damage and 0 madness.
  Melee attackers get counter-chopped.
- RHYTHM COMBO: each PERFECT timing or parry adds a stack (max 5, +8% damage each).
  A miss or an unblocked hit resets it. Shown beside the status panel.
- BATTLE RATING: S/A/B/C on the victory screen (turns taken, damage taken, perfects).
  Best ratings are saved per fight, which gives replay value.

PART 4 - GRAPHICS & FEEL
- Camera punch-zoom toward the impact on crits, kills and the Fury slam.
- Killing-blow slow-motion on the last enemy of a fight.
- Impact starbursts, enemy squash on hit, white death flash and a dissolve.
- New BATTLE TRANSITION: a diagonal red axe-slash wipe instead of a plain fade into fights.
- Low-HP red heartbeat vignette.
- Drifting ground fog on every battlefield for depth.
